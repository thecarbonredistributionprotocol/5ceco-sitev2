Find what your maintenance records leave unresolved.

A narrow review loop

A question you can trace back to the record.

01 / Screen

Surface a candidate

A bounded screen flags a possible maintenance-evidence gap for review. A flag is a question, not a finding.

02 / Inspect

Follow the sources

The Workbench links the question to the cited defect, use and workshop rows so a reviewer can reconstruct the chronology.

03 / Decide

Keep uncertainty visible

Missing or date-only information stays explicit. The reviewer decides what further evidence is needed and what, if anything, can be concluded.

Synthetic case · FN128 / DR-0038

Was the vehicle used before the defect was resolved?

The screen raises a candidate question about use on 17–18 July 2026 after a defect report on 16 July and before a recorded return-to-service claim on 19 July.

16 JulyDefect reported · defects.csv#32
17–18 JulyVehicle-use rows · vehicle_use.csv#38 and #154
19 JulyRepair, brake test, sign-off and return-to-service entries share a date.
What the export cannot settle

It does not establish whether the earlier use was permitted, whether repair and sign-off were complete before return, or the order of the 19 July entries. The records provide dates, not within-day times.

Evidence boundary

Built for review, still awaiting real-world validation.

The current Workbench is local and has been exercised on synthetic fleet-maintenance data. A separate CSV/XLSX diagnostic preflight can inspect an export’s shape; it does not establish native compatibility or prove screening performance on operational records.

  • ShownSource-linked candidate questions and explicit uncertainty in a synthetic fixture.
  • Not yet shownNative R2C compatibility, live operational reliability, measured time savings or paying customers.
  • Reviewer's roleVerify source context, request missing evidence and make any conclusion.
