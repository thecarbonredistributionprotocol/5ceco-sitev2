03 / Where this begins

Built for the decisions that follow.

We're beginning with fleet-maintenance reviews. The longer-term ambition is to make the evidence behind consequential operational decisions easier to inspect, question and verify.

For now, the work is narrower: test whether source-linked questions from existing exports are useful to the people who review them.

04 / First review
