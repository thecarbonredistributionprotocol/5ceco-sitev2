Route Proof · original results retained

Research that got us here

Earlier Route Proof work tested whether fleet records could support specific conclusions. Positive, negative and corrected results were retained. That work led to the current hypothesis: structure the evidence and preserve uncertainty before the expert makes the call.

Leeds

Correction retained. 0 of 1,482 records were independently recomputable for fuel efficiency. An earlier approximately 83% claim was retracted.

Mesa

Positive control. 110,976 eligible records supported a narrower aggregate source-reported-quantity question.

Cincinnati

Classification changed. 14 of 134 active assets changed classification under an evidence-bounded treatment.

Synthetic GPS

Unsupported total refused. Observed distance and endpoint displacement remained separate; total route distance was not established.

Madagascar

Mixed result. Consequential workbook problems were identified, but decision-triage failed.

WCC

Negative feasibility result. Required evidence could not be retrieved sufficiently to run the intended benchmark.
